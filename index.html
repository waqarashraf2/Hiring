How We Work

From the interview stage, the best candidates are selected and shared with the company. We also assist in onboarding to ensure a smooth transition.

01

Requirement Gathering

We begin by understanding the hiring needs of the company through detailed consultations.

02

Job Analysis

Our experts analyze job descriptions to identify key skills, experience, and responsibilities.

03

Talent Hunting

We search for candidates using various platforms and networks, then screen and shortlist them.

04

Interview Coordination

We schedule interviews and ensure smooth communication between candidates and clients.

05

Selection & Onboarding

We assist with final selection and ensure a smooth onboarding process for new hires.
